# Public website launch readiness

This is an implementation status record, not a legal opinion or launch approval.

## Implemented

- Vietnamese, German and English public routes, translated navigation, page content, guide articles, metadata, canonical URLs, language alternates, sitemap and crawler rules.
- The public home, Ausbildung, study, work, opportunities, tools, Document Check, services, pricing, about, partners, contact, guides and policy routes.
- Existing private account and Document Check Centre routes remain separate under the portal route group. Public tool links lead to sign-in or registration and return to `/app`.
- Private upload and account handling continues to use the existing application controls. Public pages do not expose candidate files or account data.
- Proposed services are marked as proposed, prices as indicative where shown, and all checkout flags remain disabled.
- Six localized guides cite official sources and show their source review date.
- Existing source images are retained; optimized WebP copies live under `public/images/`. Pending visual requests are recorded in `assets/image-manifest.md`.

## Owner decisions required before commercial launch

- Confirm the operating legal entity, registered address, public contact channel and accountable data controller details.
- Approve complete privacy, terms, refund/cancellation and service-specific notices with qualified counsel for relevant jurisdictions.
- Confirm service scope, delivery timing, human review capacity, eligibility, pricing, tax treatment and refund rules. Do not enable checkout before this review.
- Approve the scanner, OCR, model and hosting providers, their regions, retention and training terms, data processing agreements, security controls and candidate notices.
- Supply a verified contact inbox and decide how inquiries are handled. The present contact and partner pages intentionally do not accept submissions.
- Provide permission for any partner names, logos, testimonials or outcome statistics before publication. None are claimed by this site.
- Review each official-source guide periodically and update the displayed review date only when the content and links are rechecked.
- Decide whether analytics are needed; perform privacy/consent review before adding any tags.
